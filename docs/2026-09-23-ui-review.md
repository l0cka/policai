# UI review — policai.org and a2j.policai.org (2026-09-23)

Review of the live sites at deployed commit `2480e20` (equal to
`origin/main`). The review itself changed nothing; every item is resolved on
branch `feat/ui-refresh-2026-09-23` (see "Resolution" at the end). Screenshots are in
`~/.hermes/cache/scratch/uireview/shots/` (full page) and `crops/` (900 px
slices); `SHOT` below means that directory.

## Findings, most severe first

### 1. Raw scraped markdown in A2J signal titles — major
- **Where:** A2J `/deadlines`, `/this-week`, `/` feed; `SHOT/a-deadlines-desk.png`.
- **What:** Titles render as `PRF News\ \ \ \ August 27, 2026\ \ … ##### PRF seeking…`
  and `Legal development\ \ \ \ ### A free for all?…`. This is the most
  visible quality defect on either site.
- **Reference:** stored title shown as-is at `apps/probono/dashboard/app/deadlines.tsx:33`;
  titles come from `apps/probono/worker/src/lib/page-title.ts` (`extractPageTitle`),
  which has no markdown or escaped-space cleanup.
- **Suggested fix:** clean titles in the worker (strip `#` runs and `\ ` escapes, drop
  the leading site/date breadcrumb), plus a one-off backfill of existing rows
  (a database write, so it needs approval separately).

### 2. A2J signal-network numbers are invisible — major
- **Where:** A2J `/`, open "Explore the signal network"; `SHOT/a-signal-network-open.png`.
- **What:** In the summary row under the graph, the numbers are invisible: the `<dd>`
  inherits `rgb(32,40,34)` on the dark-green panel, a contrast ratio of 1.02:1. Only the
  labels show, and they are 8 px `#8395a8`, which is barely legible.
- **Reference:** `apps/probono/dashboard/app/globals.css:1103-1107`; markup at
  `apps/probono/dashboard/app/signal-network.tsx:396`.
- **Suggested fix:** give `.signal-network-summary dd` the panel's light text colour, and
  set the labels to at least 11 px in a lighter tone.

### 3. A2J uses 8–9 px text in eight places — major
- **Where:** A2J home (18 elements at 9 px, 4 at 8 px measured).
- **What:** Below any sensible legibility floor; the design's mono small caps are
  already hard to read at 10–11 px.
- **Reference:** `apps/probono/dashboard/app/globals.css:856,904,987,1031,1107,1165,1208,3955`.
- **Suggested fix:** set a 10 px floor (11 px for anything carrying meaning).

### 4. The two sites use different page widths — minor
- **Where:** Policai `/network`, `/policies/[id]`, `/developments` vs `/` and `/this-week`;
  `SHOT/p-network-desk.png`, `SHOT/p-policy-desk.png`.
- **What:** The header and register are capped at 1240 px, but network, policy detail,
  developments, courts, timeline and status use `.container` (1536 px), so the page
  content starts at x=32 while the logo sits at x=132. The page jumps sideways
  between tabs.
- **Reference:** `src/app/globals.css:458-461` and `:650`; `.container` used in
  `src/components/network-browser.tsx`, `developments-browser.tsx`, `courts-browser.tsx`,
  `timeline-browser.tsx`, `src/app/policies/[id]/page.tsx`, `src/app/status/page.tsx`.
- **Suggested fix:** one shared page-shell width, 1240 px, for every route.

### 5. Policai developments leaves a wide dead column — minor
- **Where:** `/developments` desktop; `SHOT/p-developments-desk.png` (crop 1).
- **What:** The list stops at about 1072 px, then a ruled empty column fills the rest.
  The source column wraps long names to three lines while that space is unused.
- **Reference:** `src/components/developments-browser.tsx`.
- **Suggested fix:** use the empty column (for example a filter rail or month index)
  or let the list take the full width.

### 6. The developments tabs are cut off on mobile — minor
- **Where:** `/developments` at 390 px; `SHOT/p-developments-mob.png`.
- **What:** The second tab reads "Automated r…". It scrolls horizontally with the
  scrollbar hidden, so users get no sign that the tab continues.
- **Reference:** `src/components/developments-browser.tsx:191` (`no-scrollbar`, `text-lg`).
- **Suggested fix:** shorter labels ("Verified" / "Unconfirmed") or a smaller size on mobile.

### 7. Network graph labels overlap in the dense cluster — minor
- **Where:** `/network` default view; `SHOT/p-network-desk.png`.
- **What:** Court-guidance labels are drawn on top of each other and over the nodes,
  so the main cluster cannot be read until the user zooms in.
- **Reference:** `src/components/network/ForceGraph.tsx` (`<text>` labels).
- **Suggested fix:** show labels only for hovered or selected nodes and their neighbours
  at the default zoom, or apply label collision avoidance.

### 8. "Overdue" fails contrast on /status — minor
- **Where:** Policai `/status`, light theme.
- **What:** `--caution` `#b7791f` at 14 px on the page background gives a 3.4:1
  contrast ratio. WCAG AA needs 4.5:1.
- **Reference:** `src/app/status/page.tsx:166`; token `src/app/globals.css:111`.
- **Suggested fix:** darken the light-mode `--caution` to about `#8a5a12`.

### 9. Status pill wraps into two lines — polish
- **Where:** Register list and policy detail; `SHOT/p-home-desk.png`, `SHOT/p-policy-desk.png`.
- **What:** "Active / Not yet in effect" is a two-line badge, heavier than every other
  pill, and in upper-case mono on the detail header.
- **Reference:** `src/components/policy-indicators.tsx:35`.
- **Suggested fix:** a single "Active · from 20 Oct" or a separate small note below the pill.

### 10. The sites handle titles and chrome differently — polish
- **What:** A2J `/deadlines`, `/health`, `/` and 404 all have the tab title "Policai A2J"
  (no per-page title), while `/this-week` uses an em dash ("This week — Policai A2J")
  and root Policai uses a hyphen. Page `<h1>` scale also differs: A2J 48 px, Policai
  30 px. The theme toggle is 72 px tall on Policai and 34 px on A2J.
  Nav naming also differs: A2J "Sources" goes to a page titled "Source health", and the
  utility bar also has a separate "Source health" link.
- **Reference:** `apps/probono/dashboard/app/layout.tsx:25`, `nav.tsx:14`;
  `apps/probono/dashboard/app/this-week/page.tsx:10`.
- **Suggested fix:** one title template ("Page - Policai A2J"), shared h1 and toggle
  sizes, and a single name for source health.

### 11. Policai "This week" looks empty — polish
- **Where:** `/this-week`; `SHOT/p-thisweek-desk.png`.
- **What:** Three paragraphs of caveats frame a "No verified developments" message.
  Most of the page is disclaimer text. A2J's "This week" shows 22 items.
- **Suggested fix:** when the window is empty, show the latest verified items with a
  note that they are older, and shorten the caveat copy.

### 12. Network page has a duplicate `<h1>` — polish
- **Where:** `/network`: two `<h1>Policy relationships</h1>` (measured).
- **Reference:** `src/app/network/` and `src/components/network-browser.tsx`.

## Not defects (checked and discarded)
- `/map`, `/framework`, `/agencies` and `/blog` return 404 by design: they were retired in
  `41fd356`. The "Project Structure" section of `AGENTS.md` still lists them, so it is out of date.
- No horizontal overflow at 390 px on any page. No unlabelled controls. No images
  without alt text. No console errors, except the expected 404s.

## Coverage
- **Covered (desktop 1440 and mobile 390, light):** Policai `/`, `/this-week`, `/developments`,
  `/courts`, `/timeline`, `/network`, `/methodology`, `/status`, a policy detail page and 404.
  A2J `/`, `/this-week`, `/deadlines`, `/sector`, `/health` and 404.
- **Covered (dark, desktop):** Policai `/` and A2J `/`, with an automated contrast scan of six pages.
  Dark mode is clean apart from item 2.
- **Not covered:** hover and focus states, keyboard navigation, the empty and error states of
  filters, and tablet widths.
- **Method:** Playwright-core with headless Chromium 1234 against production, full-page
  screenshots, DOM metrics (overflow, target sizes, font-size histogram, WCAG contrast),
  then visual inspection of native-resolution crops.
