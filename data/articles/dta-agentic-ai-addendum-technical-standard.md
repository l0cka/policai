The Digital Transformation Agency (DTA) publishes the [Agentic AI addendum to the AI technical standard for Australian Government](/policies/agentic-ai-addendum-technical-standard-2026) on [digital.gov.au](https://www.digital.gov.au/policy/ai/agentic-ai-addendum) and as a [PDF](https://www.digital.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/2026-06/Addendum-Agentic-AI.pdf), both last updated on 4 June 2026 ([addendum page](https://www.digital.gov.au/policy/ai/agentic-ai-addendum)). The page says it provides best-practice guidance for Australian Government agencies implementing agentic AI, as an addendum to the [AI technical standard](/policies/ai-technical-standard) for secure and governed implementation of agentic AI systems ([addendum page](https://www.digital.gov.au/policy/ai/agentic-ai-addendum)).

## What it says

All statements, criteria and general guidance in the AI technical standard remain applicable ([addendum page](https://www.digital.gov.au/policy/ai/agentic-ai-addendum)). Some addendum criteria may also apply to non-agentic AI, for example memory management ([addendum page](https://www.digital.gov.au/policy/ai/agentic-ai-addendum)). Agencies exploring, developing or using agentic AI are expected to apply the addendum with the technical standard ([addendum page](https://www.digital.gov.au/policy/ai/agentic-ai-addendum)). Named technologies are examples only and do not imply DTA endorsement ([addendum page](https://www.digital.gov.au/policy/ai/agentic-ai-addendum)).

The PDF adds statements AGT.1 to AGT.8 across the lifecycle: governance and safeguards (including human accountability and an orchestration layer); memory management; agentic workflow design; routing and data flow; agent, model and technology selection; continuous evaluation; tools and interaction protocols; and ongoing monitoring ([PDF](https://www.digital.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/2026-06/Addendum-Agentic-AI.pdf)). For decommissioning it points to Statement 41 of the parent standard and adds agent-specific shutdown and memory steps ([PDF](https://www.digital.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/2026-06/Addendum-Agentic-AI.pdf)).

The parent [AI technical standard](/policies/ai-technical-standard) says agencies that adopt it are accountable for its required and recommended criteria ([introduction, scope and audience](https://www.digital.gov.au/policy/ai/AI-technical-standard/technical-standard-governments-use-artificial-intelligence-introduction-scope-and-target-audience)). The [Policy for the Responsible Use of AI in Government](/policies/policy-responsible-use-ai-government-v2) lists mandatory requirements for accountable officials, transparency statements, a strategic approach, operational controls, use-case registers and impact assessments, and does not name the technical standard among those "must" items on the policy page ([policy page](https://www.digital.gov.au/ai/ai-in-government-policy)). The addendum's applicability section says agencies must follow that policy, and lists related instruments including the technical standard and ASD's [Careful adoption of agentic AI services](/policies/asd-careful-adoption-agentic-ai-services-2026) ([PDF](https://www.digital.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/2026-06/Addendum-Agentic-AI.pdf)).

## Who it affects

The landing page addresses Australian Government agencies implementing agentic AI, and says agencies exploring, developing or using agentic AI are expected to apply the addendum with the technical standard ([addendum page](https://www.digital.gov.au/policy/ai/agentic-ai-addendum)). The parent standard's audience includes AI, business, technical, development and operations roles in government, and industry partners that implement the standard for products or services they supply ([introduction, scope and audience](https://www.digital.gov.au/policy/ai/AI-technical-standard/technical-standard-governments-use-artificial-intelligence-introduction-scope-and-target-audience)).

## Analysis

In Policai's analysis, the addendum's force is the DTA's wording: agencies "are expected to apply" it with the technical standard, and the page calls the text best-practice guidance ([addendum page](https://www.digital.gov.au/policy/ai/agentic-ai-addendum)). The parent standard speaks of agencies that "adopt" it; the responsible-use policy's published mandatory list does not itself name that standard ([introduction, scope and audience](https://www.digital.gov.au/policy/ai/AI-technical-standard/technical-standard-governments-use-artificial-intelligence-introduction-scope-and-target-audience); [policy page](https://www.digital.gov.au/ai/ai-in-government-policy)).

In Policai's analysis, the open question is how the AGT statements sit next to the parent standard's required criteria and ASD's careful-adoption guidance listed as related reading ([PDF](https://www.digital.gov.au/sites/default/files/documents/2026-06/Addendum-Agentic-AI.pdf); [record](/policies/asd-careful-adoption-agentic-ai-services-2026)). The addendum does not say that applying it meets the [Policy for the Responsible Use of AI in Government](/policies/policy-responsible-use-ai-government-v2).
