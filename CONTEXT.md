# Domain terms

- **Court instrument** – a court or tribunal document governing or guiding AI use, such as a practice note, direction or judicial guideline.
- **Court requirement** – a reviewer-verified normative proposition extracted from a court instrument. It records the actor, modality, action, conditions, exceptions and topics without replacing the source document or making a legal conclusion.
- **Requirement candidate** – an AI-assisted or manually extracted proposition awaiting editorial review. Candidates are not public and are not returned to agents.
- **Requirement review** – a human decision to verify or reject a requirement candidate after comparing its structured proposition and source anchor against the current court instrument.
- **Source anchor** – the exact source quote, pinpoint locator, official URL and content hash used to verify a requirement against a particular document version.
